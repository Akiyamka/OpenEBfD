## MatchSnapshot._restore_entities()'s shape: a whole-transform write, which
## moves the node exactly as writing global_position would. The rule's pattern
## could not see it until slice R2 widened the alternation, which is how this
## one survived slice C2's migration unnoticed.

func restore(entity: Node3D, world_transform: Transform3D) -> void:
	entity.global_transform = world_transform
